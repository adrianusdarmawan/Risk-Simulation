What is moving the answer?

This is an explainable training model, not a regulatory capital model. Public evidence is used as a prior or calibration reference and must not be treated as an unbiased company-specific loss sample.

Model structure

Objective first. No black-box number.
1. Objective / KPI
2. Exposure
3. Public / scenario prior
4. Internal evidence
5. Control & change
6. Bayesian update
7. Monte Carlo
8. Objective-at-Risk

Frequency uses a Gamma-Poisson Bayesian update. Severity uses a lognormal distribution around the user's loss-per-event estimate. The app then simulates annual aggregate losses.
